The Founder

Yes, I Am The Koza Literary Liaison

Elena Koza, affectionately known as The Koza Literary Liaison, is a nationally-acclaimed book marketing expert with over 40 years of experience working with global publishers, independent imprints and first-time novelists. She is passionate about author platforms and emotional marketing strategies that deliver real value to readers and measurable impact to publishers.

Elena is a mentor, a visionary and a true master of her craft — inspiring others to reach for greatness, and helping them achieve their dreams one beautifully executed campaign at a time.
