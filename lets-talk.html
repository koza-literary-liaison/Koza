Before We Speak

Questions We Hear Often

Do you work with self-published authors?

Absolutely — roughly half our roster is independently published. We will look at your numbers first and tell you honestly whether a full campaign is the right investment, or whether a focused, lower-cost plan will do more for you right now.

When should we get started?

Six to nine months out from publication is ideal. That gives us time to build the email list, grow the author platform, place advance reviews and pre-sell the book. For a rushed release, we can still help — just less of it.

Do you work with publishers as well as authors?

Yes. We work with independent presses, hybrid publishers, literary agencies and commercial imprints of every size, on both frontlist launches and backlist revivals.

Do you take a percentage of royalties?

Some engagements are flat-fee, some are performance-based, and the right structure depends on your print run and our forecast. We will present both and let you choose. We never hide a commission inside a retainer.

What happens after I get my proposal?

You get a full launch plan: the positioning, the audience, the channel mix, the timeline, the budget and the numbers we expect to hit. Then you decide. There is no pressure and no expiry date on the proposal.
