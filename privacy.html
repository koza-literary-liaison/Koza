Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we will never do with it.

Who We Are

Koza Literary Liaison ("Koza", "we", "us") is a book marketing agency based at 418 Bathurst Street, Toronto, Ontario, Canada. We run launch campaigns, author platforms and reader communities for authors, publishers and imprints. This policy explains what happens to the information you give us through this website.

Questions about this policy, or about anything we hold about you, can be sent to support.kozaliteraryliaison@gmail.com and a member of our team will respond.

What We Collect

Information you give us. When you request a marketing proposal or complete the author registration form we collect the details you type in: your name, email address, optional phone number, company or imprint name, postal address, and the book details you share — title, genre, publication date, print run, website or social handle, the areas you are interested in, and any message you write.

Information collected automatically. Like nearly every website, we may record basic technical data such as your browser type, the pages you visit and the approximate region you are connecting from, in order to keep the site working and to understand which pages are useful.

Why We Collect It

To respond to you. The only reason we ask for your details is so we can review your book, prepare a proposal and contact you about moving forward. We use your email address to reply to your enquiry and to send the materials we promised.

To prepare a marketing plan. The book details you share tell us what we are working with — format, genre, print run, audience and goals — so the strategy we build is specific to your book rather than generic.

To improve the site. Aggregate information about how visitors use the site helps us work out which pages to improve.

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell your information. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone. Your details are used to run your campaign and for nothing else.

We do not add you to a mailing list you did not ask for. If you ask to hear from us about launches, campaigns or opportunities, that is a separate, clearly labelled permission and you can withdraw it at any time.

We do not ask for passwords, card details or bank information. There is no paid checkout on this site, and we never need financial information from you to talk about your book.

How Long We Keep It

We keep enquiry and registration details for as long as the conversation with you is live and for a reasonable period afterwards so we can pick up where we left off. If you would like us to remove your details, email us and we will do it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how we use it. We will always honour a straightforward request. Write to kozaliteraryliaison@gmail.com and we will deal with it.

Cookies And Analytics

This site is built to work without tracking cookies. We use self-hosted fonts and local images, and we do not run advertising cookies or cross-site trackers. If we ever add analytics, we will say so here and describe exactly what it measures before it goes live.

Changes And Contact

We will update this page whenever our practices change, and the date below always reflects the most recent version. For anything at all, write to kozaliteraryliaison@gmail.com.
