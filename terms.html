Terms Of Service

The straightforward terms for working with Koza Literary Liaison.

These Terms In Short

Using this website, or registering as an author through it, means you accept these terms. In plain language: we will treat your manuscript and your details with care, we will be honest with you about what marketing can and cannot do, and we will never claim ownership of your work.

Questions can go to kozaliteraryliaison@gmail.com.

About This Website

This site describes the services of Koza Literary Liaison, a book marketing agency. The content — case studies, statistics, testimonials, sample campaign figures and advice — is provided for general information. Nothing on this site is a promise of results, and no figure we publish is a guarantee of how your own book will perform.

Registration

When you complete the author registration form you are asking us to review your book and send you a proposal. Registering costs nothing, commits you to nothing and does not create a contract for services. It simply tells us you are ready to move forward, and it lets us stop sending you proposals and start building a plan.

A registration reference is issued for every completed registration so that we can find your submission quickly. Please quote it if you get in touch.

Your Rights And Warranties

When you register, you confirm that the manuscript you are describing is your own work, or that you hold the rights to market it. We rely on that confirmation, and we would ask you to indemnify us against any claim that arises from a misrepresentation on your part.

You keep every right you already had. Registering with us does not transfer ownership of your manuscript, your title, your cover or your data. We do not claim any rights over the work you send us.

How We Work With You

Once we have your details, a named strategist reviews your book and contacts you within one business day with a proposal and an invitation to a kickoff call. That proposal sets out the strategy, the channel mix, the timeline and the budget for the work. Nothing is charged until you accept a proposal in writing, and until you do, there is no obligation on either side.

If we cannot do the work well, we will say so and, where we can, point you to someone who can.

Confidentiality

Manuscripts, synopses, cover art, sales figures and campaign plans you share with us are confidential. We do not publish them, we do not use them as case studies, and we do not show them to other clients, without your written permission. The exception is anonymous aggregate data used to measure our own performance.
